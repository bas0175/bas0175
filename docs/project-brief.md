# Obsidian Cycle III: Pilgrimage, project brief

Context for anyone (or any session) working on the game. The game-specific handoff for v31 is `handoff-v31.md`.

## The dissertation
- *The Obsidian Cycle* is the dissertation project. It has three pieces. The pilgrimage cycles inside the game are a separate thing.
- Claim: single-author intermedia works, like modern worldbuilding, are a simulacrum of a compressed network, moving from distributed production to single authorship. The proof is cross-media comparison plus a measurable severance test of network signatures.
- VELTA (Vertices, Edges, Layers, Temporal dynamics, Attributes) makes the world's latent network explicit. The Children of the Moon ritual generates the network the other two pieces are built from. The game's JSON graph is a placeholder until that graph exists; it then drives the puzzles (who, what pitch, what precedes or overlaps what). VELTA mainly affects the puzzles and the Assembly sections.
- **Piece I, Children of the Moon:** a theatrical oratorio of about 30 minutes, performed 2 to 3 times in succession. Premieres April 2027 at MEIT.
- **Piece II, Relics of Shadow:** a 1 to 2 day gallery installation of ritual objects and instruments with interactive sound sculpture. It uses the same aesthetic sheets as the game.
- **Piece III, Pilgrimage (this game):** embodied reenactment through gameplay. The player performs sections of the rite seen in Piece I, closing the loop. Primarily the artwork; it is evidence because it is generated in part from the same network and wholly from the same worldbuilding.
- Sound, ritual vocabulary and instruments should match Piece I. Piece I's score is separate but informs the game.
- Play logs: for now, the author's own observation and analysis.

## Deadline and audience
- Hard deadline for the game: mid January 2027.
- Audience: the committee, the public at the recital, and later participants. The author may play it live (suggested by the chair), and others should be able to play it too.
- Format: browser, single HTML file today. Open to change.

## The Survanic world
- An unexplained Cataclysm covered the planet in obsidian. The Survaen are the survivors, about 300 people in a cave network. They revere stone; cavern acoustics shape their culture.
- The Assembly is the main community. Outposts sit 1 to 2 days' journey out. Once per cycle (length unknown) each outpost sends one representative on pilgrimage: leave, travel, check in, Assembly, gather item(s), return.
- A food shortage now affects the whole community, not only the outposts.
- The light: what pilgrims carry home from the Assembly. Its literal and symbolic meaning is still being developed.

## Characters
- **Veyra:** the protagonist, the outpost leader's daughter. Later High Priestess Veyra Anurin; Anurin, "the returning," is earned in her lifetime. Her ascension is the moment she takes the rite and joins Kreven as co-leader, the start of her path to High Priestess and reformer of the Order of Shadows.
- **Father:** the outpost leader. His hand-drawn map, with its intentionally outdated note, opens the deep ways.
- **Torven:** earned name, "the one who waits." An older community administrator who keeps things running and becomes her quiet patron. The fungi discovery raises her standing with him.
- **Kreven:** leads the rite now and wields the Vorrakiel. Later her right hand and Sorenai.

## Look
- Reference sheets are in `style/`. Ponchos and layered dark cloth with amber-orange geometric hems, wrapped forearms, buns, boots; slate-blue stone; cold white light on inscribed stone; amber only on lamps, struck stones and the light.
- Open to any art approach that fits the aesthetic and narrative.

## Direction (October 2026)
- Testing 2.5D: real 3D cave rendered with Three.js, side-on platforming unchanged, characters as 2D sprites in the 3D world. Rule: depth is scenery, never gameplay, so the game can collapse back to 2D.
- All sounds must be swappable, so Piece I recordings can replace the synthesized placeholders.
- v31 is preserved untouched in `v31/`.
